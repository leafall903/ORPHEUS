Implementation blockers
-----------------------
nodes.TrackTargets currently returns an empty tuple and performs no acquisition.
Sombra0B1B has three such states; EMP0B31/node40 and Camo0C9B/node128 also
contain searches. Their full filters, outputs, timing and engine role policy
cannot be replaced by a generic enemy-in-radius loop without losing authored
health-pack, immunity and status semantics. The legacy passive-State fallback
does not prove the additional entity/status actions have any server effect.
Combat._damage currently has no hero barrier, reflection, absorption or immunity
service. It changes pools directly. Those cross-hero requirements are unaccepted.
The parent owns actual five-NPC creation/control; its integration is still open
at this lane's baseline. The existing retain_corpse opt-in alone is not five-NPC
combat acceptance. No client or normal service was controlled by this lane.
