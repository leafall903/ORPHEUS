Stop/death/control/body/world invalidation clears a pending search. A pending
search samples the target pose until it completes, avoiding repeated restarts;
a moving target is resampled afterward. This can delay moving-target pursuit
and is not native perception or full moving-target combat acceptance.
Edges require supported floor, slope, headroom and capsule-width static rays.
The planning step-up bound is the existing manual bot0.6m policy; actual poses
are advanced through the shared Mover and CharacterBody contact/sweep engine.
The old bot floor-following route alone has no lateral wall sweep and is not
used as the manual physical controller. Missing floor is not a walkable bridge.

Each body uses its loaded mover dimensions/speeds, acceleration and turn policy.
Routes are revalidated, world/body changes rebuild the physical mover, and
duplicate frames do not move again. Catch-up retains the existing bot625-tick
maximum. Paused ticks do not replay their movement on recovery. Stop/death clears
path and movement; explicit removal releases the navigator while keeping the
existing retained-corpse policy until that removal. Movement records preserve
input direction against the final weapon-aim yaw. Navigator/mover/path/target/
planning time and progress, including the open frontier, costs/parents/visited
nodes and sampled target, are explicit causal server snapshot inputs.
