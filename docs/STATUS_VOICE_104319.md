# Status and damage reactions in build 104319

A status applied to a body, a voice criterion reported by a graph and an audible line are three separate observations. The table records the first two from the local 104319 installation. Hearing a cue requires the graph to reach its voice action, the selected actor and skin to supply an eligible line, and the room to deliver it to the listener. Original-client playback remains open for the rows below.

Graph and node pairs use hexadecimal graph IDs and decimal `nodes[].pos` from the prepared bundle. Four-digit IDs in the criterion and stimulus columns are the low resource indices of `.01C` and `.078`, respectively. Named stimuli use the pinned OWLib GUID catalog.

## Status inputs and line eligibility

The local runtime generator inspected 65 `.053` definitions. In 41, the top-level `STU_76F2C784.m_5A4051BC` refers directly to a `.01B` script. The other 24 have a null on-apply field. The separate `status_scripts_174.json` records those 41 pairs. Its contents are graph bindings; audio, effects and rendering payloads remain in the client installation.

The 950-graph local bundle and the 104319 map-script ZIP both contain the relevant shared and hero scripts, including `01CA`, `01C8`, `01C9`, `224E`, `0BDE`, `0B77`, `1502` and `150F`. None of the four standalone Training Room scripts in that ZIP contains a direct `STUStatescriptActionCriteria` or `STU_40E05F6B` voice action. The inspected reactions originate in the shared body, ability and status scripts.

There is a second eligibility check inside individual voice sets. For example, Reaper's stock `.05F` has active 00B6, 00B7 and 0284 rows whose own `STUCriteria_Statescript` references `191E` with `m_57D96E27 = 1`. That differs from the criterion which requests the stimulus. The inspected pseudocode includes `191E` states in `026A` and `0276`. The flag polarity and its lifetime on the speaking actor still need a matched observation. The runtime generator keeps such directly state-conditioned rows separate from currently playable rows. Comparing another hero's result does not resolve that condition.

To verify one row in a room, record the source and affected body generations, the applied status or damage tags, the graph node, the actor resolved by its target expression, and the chosen stimulus and line. Pair the room trace with an original-client observation of the same event.
