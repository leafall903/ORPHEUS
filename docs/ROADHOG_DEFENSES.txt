Only these four zero-delay54FEB21C entries are advanced on actual player combat

are checked before request_finish; the authored graph then owns duration/end.
Strict VM execution is not overridden. Native8E5A90/8E5F50 evidence from the

Reconstructed combat policy
- Wraith/Fade bodies are omitted from enemy hit candidates. Pellets, close slug,
  expired secondary fragments, hitscan Whole Hog and Hook can reach a rear enemy.
- Cryo/Transcendence retain their ordinary capsule and intercept shots/Hook.
  Actual protected damage is rejected before stats, notices, healing deficit or
  ultimate gain. Hook ends without damage/control and does not stop the defender's
  protection graph. Normal weapon ammo and Hook cooldown still apply.
- Mapped protection rejects the shared Flashbang status callback. This is scoped
  control immunity, not generic invulnerability or a cleanse implementation.
- Damage/protection checks use live impact-time state. An already launched
  projectile observes a cast started before arrival. Expiry, Mei's real primary
  cancel and body replacement restore ordinary damage/collision admission.
- Exporting an active cast is read-only: no variable evaluation or callback
  reentry is permitted by the regression. Server/model manifest coverage includes
  the adapter's exact mapping and existing authored state storage.

Direct evidence
Real Roadhog primary, close slug, secondary fragments, melee, Hook and running
Whole Hog are tested against all four actual defensive casts. Tests retain high
health targets for repeated damage; this does not alter retail hero definitions.
Real Flashbang immunity, expiry/replacement, in-flight transitions, Mei cancel,
ammo/cooldown/charge rejection and active snapshot checks supplement these cases.

Open scope
Native hitbox/phase/filter defaults, exact cast simultaneity and historical phase
lag compensation, cleanse, every control type, all barriers/reflect/absorb and
other immunity sources remain open. NPCs receive no inferred protection from
these human hero mappings. Other heroes' complete kits are not accepted here.
No original-client HUD, effects, animation, sound or prediction acceptance.
