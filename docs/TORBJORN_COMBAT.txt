Implemented here
----------------
Projectile direct impacts now use the evaluated HitDef distance falloff and
explicit critical multiplier. The hit flag follows the explicit multiplier;
blast damage is unaffected. Distance is flown time times the constant speed of
the existing straight-line solver. Critical hit geometry remains the existing
capsule/eye approximation, not an authored hitbox reconstruction.

Actual 1683/st13 per-pellet damage is 12.5, full through 10m, declining to 30%
at 20m. Tests measure player pool changes at 5m/15m/25m, and the explicit
critical multiplier/flag. The primary 1683/st10 volley damages a player once
for 70. Torbjorn's initial 200 health plus 50 armour accepts opposing player
damage, then dies with owner_dead and killer identity recorded.

Do not infer native headshot defaults from these tests: both extracted Torbjorn
hit definitions omit m_A872C70E, so the current evaluator defaults to 1. The
critical test explicitly sets a multiplier of 2 to test the shared impact path.
