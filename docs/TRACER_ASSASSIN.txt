Tracer versus manual Assassin: partial server reconstruction
==========================================================

This lane executes the actual01BB body and1157 graph against Tracer. It does
not establish complete Assassin AI, native controller allocation or client
presentation. Full Tracer static acceptance and all original-client acceptance
remain open. Changes are uncommitted until the complete static objective passes.

Controller and health context
-----------------------------
Manual Assassin spawning reserves a body ID and a distinct server-only
controller ID in the existing monotonic NPC allocator. DB742151 returns an
Entity, never a Map or the body itself. The declared controller's foreign1650
member supplies the current player BODY Entity. The actual graph stores this
in local536. Other graphs and undeclared contexts retain unsupported behavior.
There is no fabricated controller entity on the wire. Native possession,
component layout, acquisition and allocation semantics remain unverified.

Manual input selects button36; the extracted default20 does not reach this pin.
The graph supplies25 contact damage, a delayed100 amount with mode1, and scope
exits. Treating mode1 as damage per second on elapsed state time is reconstructed.
ModifyHealth records last_applied; repeated host updates cannot repeat a tick.
The pin registry rejects Tracer movement and ability input through the shared
interrupt path, preserving weapon/ability press cleanup and authored exits.

Victims must be connected, living enemy player bodies matching the explicit
manual target. Sources must be living, firing, un-stunned and retain the same
component. A3m eye-distance and capsule LOS check is manual contact policy.
Stop, removal, source death/stun, target invalidation and scope end release the
registry and health clock. Explicit reengagement exits unresolved releaseAnim31;
its native duration and automatic recovery are still open.

An actual projected Particle Barrier absorbs the 25-point contact
and the continuous damage while the manual pin still locks Tracer's
input. When cast on an already pinned Tracer, a projected barrier
with one point left absorbs the breaking tick, credits 0.2 energy,
and leaves later ticks to damage Tracer. Both damage phases forward
the extracted 302F damage tag to Combat.

An actual Winston dome crossing probe places the Assassin outside
and Tracer inside with the shield surface between them. The current
host applies contact and pin while the dome stays at 700. The
original-client barrier admission for this direct health and control
effect is still open.

Lethal contact and lethal continuous damage each clear the pin and
credit the Assassin. Actual Mercy Resurrect restores the same Tracer
body. The retained manual engagement waits through 00CB damage
protection and applies a fresh contact on the first unprotected
frame. The earlier end of the rising lock lets Tracer Blink while
the damage protection is still active.

Normal respawn and live same-hero replacement retire the old Tracer
body. The retained manual engagement approaches the new spawn and
deals fresh contact damage. If two replacements reuse the old body
ID in one frame, retirement ends the old scope and processes its exit
before the new binding. The first hit is 25 contact damage. Before
the fix, the old 1.6 tick carried over.

An active pin settles elapsed damage on its combat tick, including
before the normal retained frame window. With six health left,
contiguous updates and 24- or 100-frame gaps kill on the fourth
frame with matching Assassin attribution and respawn deadline.
A new pin begins within the retained window. With an actual Sound
Barrier reduced to nine shield and six base health, continuous and
100-frame updates both kill on the sixth frame after decay and pin
damage. These comparisons use static retained poses.

An actual Nano Boost halves the pin's 25-point contact. Its active
continuous ticks deal 0.8 until Nano expires, then 1.6 starting
on the expiry frame. Contiguous, 24-frame and 100-frame updates
give the same damage sequence and final state.

An actual allied Sleep Dart ends an active pin. Tracer's pistols
damage the sleeping Assassin. Damage in the initial protection
window leaves the sleep deadline intact. Damage at the end of
that window caps the remaining sleep at 0.5 seconds. The retained
engagement starts a new pin at the shortened deadline.

Pulse Bomb survives a pin that begins after release, both in flight
and after attachment. With a projected barrier protecting Tracer,
the blast kills a wounded 350-health Assassin, clears the pin and
allows a fresh Blink. The same blast leaves a full-health Assassin
at 145 with the pin still active. At close range without the
projected barrier, the blast kills both bodies. Tracer retains kill
attribution on the Assassin, and her own death is self-attributed.

The same-frame probe starts one frame before detonation, during
active continuous pin damage. At 0.5 health the pin kills Tracer
before the blast. At 2 health the pin leaves a fraction and the
blast kills Tracer. Pulse kills the Assassin on that frame in both
cases, with Tracer attribution. Contiguous updates and 24- or
100-frame gaps agree on both deaths, the cleared pin and the
respawn deadline.

Pursuit
-------
Only manually spawned Assassin pursuit is connected to Match updates here.
The reviewed parent's collision-aware incremental grid router is reconstructed:
1m grid,2048 visited-node bound,4 heap pops/update,30-tick planning interval and
2.5m standoff. It checks floor, headroom and capsule width and uses Mover physics.
The actual capsule/speed come from prepared XML-derived mover data:1.5m height,
0.5m radius,14m/s forward speed. Missing Assassin mover metadata stops pursuit.
All other NPC routes retain their previous Match behavior. Native navigation,
lunges, jumping, QTEs and obstacle tactics remain open.

The5m flat-world combat probe reaches25 contact damage on frame10 with these
authored mover values. This does not accept real-map navigation or deployment.

Shared Ability buffering
------------------------
A parent-reviewed generic bug refreshed an early press timestamp every unready
tick. Actual Tracer tests reproduced released Recall auto-recast after its real
12s cooldown and unfunded Pulse auto-launch after later charge fill. Ability
now retains the original timestamp while unready and consumes it when ready.
Five actual Tracer fixtures pass expiry, held input and recent short buffering.
The shared fix adds no causal fields and preserves stop/keep consumption.
