about 0.95 seconds after stop, despite the observer retirement ACK
at 142 ms. McCree stays at 175 HP during the stopped interval and
drops to 125 HP after the new volley. Client-side muzzle effect
cancellation remains open.

projection omitted the chase (/11), the animation and stack (/9 and /0),

value (instance variable 1590) to zero. Four idle and stopped comparisons
showed the glow only after engagement, even with that reset and no

separates the bounded client presentation from a persistent active weapon.

after 62 recovery updates. The first new volley deals 50 damage only
to the replacement player. The previous cancellation path leaves the
old windup active in the console fixture. These cases use simulated
transport and cover server targeting and damage.
