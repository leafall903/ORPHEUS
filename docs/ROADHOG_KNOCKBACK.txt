Movement and lifetime
---------------------
External motion is independent of ordinary locomotion velocity, so the next input
does not erase it with the normal run-speed cap. Every world tick sweeps the real
character capsule through Mover.displace / CharacterBody.move and clips against
contact planes. It does not teleport through walls. Player movement records expose
the sum of ordinary and external velocity. NPCs use their actual body mover data,
an independent Mover for neutral gravity/contact progression, and publish their
updated authoritative position and velocity.

Horizontal external velocity uses linear damping from decel_ground_base times
run_forward (the mover's base acceleration units). Applying that ground-base rule
to external velocity in air is reconstructed. Upward boost damps by the mover's
gravity scale times world gravity; ordinary movement still owns its normal fall.
The channel is substepped at16ms, with a bounded128-step recovery per update.
For the authored default caps it stops inside that bound. Repeated world ticks
spend no extra movement. Input-free external-channel catch-up matches continuous
world updates. Normal player gravity/prediction under arbitrary command loss is
not established by that narrow equivalence test.

Source channel cancellation does not revoke inertia already delivered. Receiver
death, body retirement, removal, control immunity and Hook takeover retire the
motion. Knockback alone adds no stun or ability cancellation; real receiver
Breather/Whole Hog scopes are checked independently. Native per-hit caps,
aggregation, mass, tank reductions, control-tag priorities, friction/airborne
combination, history/clock ordering and client prediction remain parity work.
