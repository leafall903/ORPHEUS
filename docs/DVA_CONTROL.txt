D.Va delayed timed-control input
--------------------------------
Flashbang, Sleep Dart and freeze keep a bounded history of their input
locks by body. The host compares late command frame IDs with half-open
world-frame intervals after the live effect ends. A blocked command
advances the script without attack or directional input. A late
freeze command also preserves the recorded aim. Commands older than the
retained history advance neutrally. The live world tick still owns
movement effects, projectile flight and damage.

Damage-triggered wake shortens the sleep lock. Projected Barrier
cleanse closes the freeze lock at application, preserving history up
to that frame. Re-exposure or retirement clears a body's old command
history. Clock admission and history retention are host rules.

Both D.Va forms accept fresh fire at the release frame and reject
delayed fire and directional input from earlier frames. The 12 cases
cover Flashbang, aged Flashbang beyond retention, natural sleep,
damage-triggered wake, natural freeze and Projected Barrier cleanse.
The Mei fixture seeds the authored minimum setting at 1. The passive
snapshot distinguishes expired freeze history and its aim from the
live status. The final suite also rechecks initial mech routing with
the actual eye origin and a body-hit aim. See
../dva-delayed-control-history-final.txt, 8 tests passed.

Delayed input after forced movement and pins
--------------------------------------------
Chain Hook opens a body input lock on admitted contact. Recovery,
caster interruption and body retirement close it at the authoritative
release tick. A barrier that takes the hook before body contact leaves
no victim lock. The history only filters late commands and uses the
existing live hook for displacement.

Assassin opens a pin lock on the victim's active body. Multiple pin
sources share the lock, so retiring one source leaves it open until
the final source releases. Released intervals remain available to
filter delayed input. Pin contact, hook movement and clock admission
remain host reconstructions.

Both forms reject delayed input after natural Hook recovery, caster
replacement and the last of two live Assassin pins. Fresh input
regains fire. The final eight-test suite also covers caster death,
Projected Barrier before Hook contact, pin-source retirement, body
replacement and Graviton separation. See
../dva-forced-control-release-final.txt. Active pin and expired lock
storage checks passed in ../dva-forced-control-storage.txt.
