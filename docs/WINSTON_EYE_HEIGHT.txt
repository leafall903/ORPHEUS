Winston and enemy McCree FirstPerson eye geometry -- build104319
==============================================================

Original1C0.003 FirstPerson m_E29993A5=1.5 and m_816B830F=1.05 provide
Winston's standing/crouched offsets. Original02A3.003 gives McCree1.425/.9975.
The former shared1.55/1.15 defaults were incorrect for these bodies.

Combat.eye_height now supplies both player attack origins and Target.eye.
Other unrecorded bodies retain existing defaults, including Soldier's recorded
crouched offset1.085. NPC origins are not inferred from absent FirstPerson data.

Three new methods verify both bodies' source/target agreement under actual
crouch commands, unchanged unspecified-body fallback, and actual Winston Tesla
casts with cover2.5cm above/below his standing and crouched eye. Standing target
fixtures use elevated feet so the target capsule axis includes the firing height;
crouched fixtures retain ground feet. Thus the cover cases distinguish the old
incorrect heights through health effects, rather than merely comparing constants.

The two original XML inputs and SHA256 values are recorded outside Git in the
lane's eye-height-resource-evidence.json. Raw game assets remain external/read
only. Camera/muzzle animation and native skeletal critical-hit regions are not
proved by these static offsets. Existing HEAD_BELOW remains reconstructed.
