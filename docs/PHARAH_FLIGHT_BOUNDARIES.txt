Depleted fuel restart

  10 fuel and the authored 0.2 s jump wait. Thrust resumes 13 command frames
  after the threshold and can spend the recovered fuel down to zero again.
- Releasing jump during the wait cancels it. A fresh jump starts another wait.
  Switching to secondary with enough fuel starts thrust on that command.
- Releasing secondary before the threshold keeps thrust off across recharge.
  A new secondary press starts immediately above the threshold and waits if
  pressed below it.

  Held thrust and fuel debit continue through the hit and end on release.

Development findings and limits
The first exploratory room incorrectly assigned Mover.world while retaining its
old CharacterBody, so its roof did not participate in contacts. Correct fixtures
use the existing Mover.use(world=...) lifecycle, rebuilding the physical body;
do not call that initial probe a production penetration bug. Two initial fuel
assertions assumed jump matched secondary or entered hover on the first grounded
command. Executed traces exposed that unsupported assumption; separate cases now
verify grounded jump/released recovery and secondary's fuel gate. Slopes,
native input/force/phase ordering, resistance and all remaining
control/cross-hero/NPC interactions remain open. These five cases do not close the
full movement action, static matrix, goal, client gate or commit gate.

Pet execution remains a separate open frontier. Reuse of existing PlayScript
request/queue code alone supplies neither authoritative Pet creation/destruction
nor cross-entity messages, active pose, damage pools or their stored snapshots.
No Pet service was fabricated to pass the known failing shield probe this turn.
